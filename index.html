How to read this sandbox

Inspired by Gelbox and its separation of sample, simulation, and rendering. No Gelbox code or assets are copied.

This is a reduced teaching model, not a calibrated instrument or a predictor of experimental retention. IEX uses pH versus pI and a heuristic salt threshold. IMAC assumes an accessible His tag and an illustrative imidazole threshold. SEC uses a log-mass approximation for globular proteins between 5–300 kDa, with void volume 8 mL and total volume 20 mL.

UV is shown in illustrative mAU with equal mass response for all proteins. Gels show monomer mass and relative amount; structures, complexes, extinction differences, binding capacity, pressure, conductivity, gradient delay, and real resin selectivity are not modeled. Selected fractions report mass purity, not activity.

Sequence estimates use residue masses and a simple independent-site pKa model. pI depends on the model and environment. Extinction estimates assume reduced cysteines. No structure is predicted. SEC depends on hydrodynamic size, shape, and oligomerization; IEX depends on surface charge as well as net charge.

The flow diagram is an original drawing based on the supplied Cytiva screenshot, showing inlet selection, separate pump tubing, mixing, sample-loop injection, column and bypass lines, detectors, and outlet routing. The injection valve is shown in a fixed inject position and the column is online. Manual loading and bypass are not operational controls. This is not an operating guide for a specific ÄKTA model; exact routing and resin parameters still require manufacturer-manual verification.

Reference destinations: Cytiva · Gelbox source. A useful future fourth method is hydrophobic interaction chromatography (HIC).
